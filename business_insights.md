
# Customer Support Analysis – Business Insights

## 1. Dataset Overview
- Total records: 8,469
- Closed tickets: 2,769 (32.7%)
- Open tickets: 2,819 (33.3%)
- Pending customer response: 2,881 (34.0%)

## 2. Key Findings

### Ticket Status
Pending customer response represents the largest status category at 34%.

### Ticket Categories
Refund requests are the most common ticket type, accounting for 1,752 tickets.

### Customer Satisfaction
The average satisfaction rating is approximately 2.99 out of 5, based on 2,769 rated records.

### Support Channels
Email is the most frequently used channel, with 2,143 tickets.

Chat has the highest average satisfaction rating at 3.08 out of 5.

## 3. Business Recommendations

- Review pending tickets to identify opportunities to improve follow-up.
- Investigate the high volume of refund requests.
- Study successful practices in chat support.
- Monitor customer satisfaction across ticket categories.
- Improve completeness of customer feedback collection.

## 4. Data Limitations

- Satisfaction ratings are available for only 2,769 records.
- Resolution time is missing for many tickets.
- Open and pending tickets should not be treated as resolved cases.
- Further validation of date and time fields is required.
